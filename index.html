About

I'm a curiosity-driven researcher exploring how data and training shape large language models—and how the brain represents language. I graduated from BITS Pilani (B.E. ECE + M.Sc. Mathematics, minor in Data Science) and am a Research Assistant at the Data Innovation & AI Lab, UC Berkeley, working with Dr. Abhishek Nagaraj and Dr. Serina Chang. I also collaborate with the Center for Human-Compatible AI (CHAI) at Berkeley, and was a student researcher at MIT CSAIL with Dr. Boris Katz and David Mayo, working on gradient-informed data selection.

In collaboration with researchers at Berkeley, I led ORQA, an occupation-indexed benchmark for LLM professional assistance; co-built an SAE and linear-probe interpretability-and-steering pipeline for LLM social-simulation agents on Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct; and study how access to pirated books (the Books3 dataset) shapes LLM performance via a "name-cloze" evaluation across 12k+ titles.

I completed my Master's thesis at Harvard's Kreiman Lab, under the supervision of Dr. Gabriel Kreiman. I analyzed intracranial iEEG during language tasks, built decoders for grammatical/semantic features, and co-designed a real-time memory-retrieval system with frontier LLMs.

Throughout my college journey, I worked with Prof. Snehanshu Saha, Prof. Amalin Prince, and Prof. Yuvaraj Rajamanickam at NTU Singapore on EEG-based emotion recognition and vision-based activity detection. I have also worked with Prof. Ganapathy Krishnamurthi at IIT Madras on implementing object detection and instance segmentation architectures.

Research interests: understanding and improving foundation models through mechanistic interpretability, data-efficient training, and rigorous evaluation, with applications to language and multimodal reasoning.

In my free time, you'll likely find me playing the violin or engaging in other music-related activities. I also enjoy playing chess and was an active member of the Institute chess team.

Experience

UC Berkeley – Data Innovation and AI Lab
Research Assistant · Berkeley, California, USA · Aug 2025 – Present

  • Led ORQA (first author), a source-grounded benchmark evaluating LLM knowledge across professional occupations. Developed an agentic question-generation and verification pipeline using O*NET and BLS data, producing 480 source-traceable questions across 116 occupations, and evaluated 15 frontier and open-weight models. Under review at ICLR 2027.
  • Developed an interpretability and steering pipeline combining sparse autoencoders and linear probes to investigate internal representations of Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct agents. Linear probes achieved 82% accuracy at layer 48, and interventions along probe directions gave dose-dependent control of agent decisions, moving the lottery switching point from about 125 to between 30 and 200 tokens. Under review at ICLR 2027.
  • Led GlobeBench (first author), a source-grounded benchmark evaluating geographic assistance across 600 scenarios in 146 cities. Models performed well on factual place questions but reached only 34.9% accuracy on route-constrained tasks, against a 25% random baseline. Accepted at the GenAI4World Workshop, COLM 2026.
  • Multi-agent exploration (in progress): studied how sharing results shapes exploration in three-agent LLM teams playing an innovation game adapted from Ederer and Manso. In preliminary experiments, sharing narrowed the team's search (24 vs. 41 distinct strategies tried) and lowered its best outcome, while raising each agent's own best result (66% to 76% of optimum). Included in a talk at Governing Agent Swarms, Stanford (Oct 2026).
  • Conducting research on how access to pirated books (Books3 dataset) shapes large language model performance, using a novel "name cloze" evaluation across 12,000+ books and causal identification based on publication-year variation. NBER Working Paper 33598.
  • Embedded ~20k books from ~200 publishers (Keepa) to map semantic market positioning; novel Positioning Measures beat genre dummies (e.g., ~34% review variance), revealing Big Five cross-genre hubs and challenger advantages.
  • Advisors: Dr. Abhishek Nagaraj, Dr. Serina Chang.
